# Release history

## v1.0.1 — 2026-11-20

Updated the manuscript version 4.3 submission documents and their export tools.

- Added editable English Word versions of the main manuscript and Supplement 1, alongside Markdown and PDF versions.
- Included the author-supplied affiliation and contact email, no-funding and no-conflict declarations, and the AI-assisted language-polishing statement.
- Completed references [11]–[13], corrected first-citation order for Tables 6–7, and aligned main/supplement figure and table references.
- Updated the English supplement's availability statement and included all 18 archived sinusoidal matched-grid comparisons as Table S19. Limiting and adverse results remain included.
- Retained the four main figures and seven supplementary figures without modifying image contents.
- Added reproducible PDF/Word export commands, provenance updates, and document validation reports.

The manuscript remains version 4.3. Trained checkpoints, field exports, result tables, training procedures, and prediction/evaluation behavior are unchanged from v1.0.0. The supplement's citation to v1.0.0 identifies the original code-and-data snapshot used for its results; v1.0.1 adds the revised submission documents. The Python package and citation metadata version are updated to 1.0.1 for this release.

## v1.0.0

Initial public reproducibility package, including 12 selected checkpoints, 288 primary-resolution field exports, fixed geometry manifests, original figures, and archived results. Publication validation covered all 12 checkpoints on the independent Euler and sinusoidal test sets; it did not repeat all FDTD simulations or full training runs.
